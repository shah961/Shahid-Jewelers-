Home / Heritage & Craftsmanship

Every piece still passes through the same three hands it did in 1980.

Shahid Jewellers began as a single counter in Peshawar's Sarafa Bazar. The bench practices that opened that counter — hand-carving, hand-setting, hand-polishing — are the same ones our karigars use today.

Our journey

From one counter in Sarafa Bazar to three showrooms across Pakistan.

1980

A counter opens in Sarafa Bazar

Founder Shahid Ullah Khan opens a single jewellery counter at Asami Gate, Peshawar, specialising in hand-carved 22K bridal pieces for families across the old city.

1996

The Peshawar showroom expands

Rising demand for diamond-set bridal work leads to a full showroom floor and an in-house stone-setting workshop, the first of its kind on the street.

2004

Karachi branch opens

A second showroom opens near Bilal Masjid, opposite Kakri Ground, bringing the same purity standards to families in Karachi.

2015

Islamabad branch opens

A showroom in Sector F-10 Markaz brings Shahid Jewellers within reach of the capital, alongside a dedicated custom-order consultation room.

Today

Three showrooms, one standard

Every branch shares the same purity certification process, the same buyback guarantee, and karigars trained under the same original bench methods.

Artisanship & process

Four stages, each done by hand, before a piece ever reaches the showroom floor.

01

Melting

Raw gold is weighed and melted in small batches to the exact karat ordered, with purity verified before shaping begins.

02

Hand-carving

Karigars carve the base form and filigree by hand, referencing the sketch agreed with the customer during consultation.

03

Stone-setting

Diamonds and gemstones are seated and secured under magnification, checked twice for alignment and grip.

04

Polishing

A final hand-polish brings out the shine before the piece is hallmark-tested and certified for sale.

Quality assurance & certification

What "certified" actually means on a Shahid Jewellers receipt.

21K & 22K Purity Standards

Every piece is tested against Pakistan Gold & Jewellers Association purity benchmarks before it is priced.

Diamond Grading

Diamonds above 0.25 carat are graded on cut, clarity and colour, with the grade recorded on your certificate.

Ethical Sourcing

Gold is sourced from vetted local refiners with documented chain-of-custody records available on request.
