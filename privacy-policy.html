Privacy Policy

1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly, including your name, phone number, email address, preferred showroom branch, and any message content, when you:

  • Submit the contact form or custom order form on this website
  • Message us via WhatsApp, phone, or email
  • Request an appointment or private consultation
  • Make a purchase or place a custom order in-store

We also automatically collect limited technical information, such as browser type, approximate location derived from IP address, and pages visited, to keep the site secure and understand general usage patterns.

2. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to inquiries and provide price quotes
  • Process custom orders, including tracking design details and weight preferences
  • Schedule and confirm appointments
  • Maintain purchase and certification records for warranty and buyback purposes
  • Improve our website and in-store service

3. WhatsApp and phone communication

When you use a WhatsApp or call link on this site, you are taken to WhatsApp or your phone's dialer to contact our desk directly. That conversation is subject to WhatsApp's own privacy practices in addition to ours. We retain WhatsApp conversation records for customer service and order-tracking purposes.

4. Custom order design uploads

Reference images you upload when requesting a custom design are used solely to brief our design team and karigars, and are retained only for the duration of your order plus a reasonable follow-up period, after which they are deleted.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with trusted service providers who help operate our website or process payments, under confidentiality obligations, or when required by law or to protect the safety of our customers and staff.

6. Data retention

Purchase and certification records are retained for as long as needed to honour warranty and buyback guarantees. Contact form and inquiry submissions not converted into an order are retained for a limited period and then deleted or anonymised.

7. Security

We apply reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information we hold, both on this website and in showroom records. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we encourage customers to avoid sharing sensitive financial details over unencrypted channels.

8. Your rights and choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you by contacting us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe and in accordance with applicable law.

9. Cookies and analytics

This website may use minimal cookies or similar technologies to remember basic preferences and measure aggregate site usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect some site features.

10. Children's privacy

Our website and showroom services are intended for adults making jewellery purchases and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.

12. Contact us

For any privacy-related questions or requests, reach us at contact@shahidjewellers.com or call +92-91-2213876.
